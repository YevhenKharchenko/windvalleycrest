Blocks, music, and the kind of fun that just clicks

So I downloaded HyperBlocks thinking it'd be just another puzzle game, but damn, was I wrong. You don't need to think about it. No heavy rules, no exhausting strategy, no pressure to “master” something complicated in ten seconds. You open it, the music begins, the colors settle in, and suddenly you’re in that smooth little zone where placing blocks becomes strangely satisfying. One row clears, then another, and before long the whole thing starts to move with rhythm.
