How to play HyperBlocks

  • 1

    Drag the blocks onto the board

    Each piece has its own shape, and your goal is to place it where it helps you build full rows or columns.

  • 2

    Fill lines to clear them

    When a row or column is complete, it disappears and gives you more room to keep playing.

  • 3

    Keep the board open

    The better your placements, the longer your run lasts. Good flow comes from balancing quick clears with smart space management.

  • 4

    Follow your rhythm

    You can play fast and aggressive, or slow and steady. HyperBlocks leaves that choice to you.

  • 5

    Switch modes when you want

    Move between Fast Mode and Un-timed Mode depending on your mood and the kind of session you want.
