Two ways to play, depending
on the day you’re having

  • Fast Mode

    This is where the energy rises. Fast Mode is for players who like movement, quick decisions, and that little pulse of urgency when the board starts demanding more from you. It’s perfect for short sessions, score chasing, and anyone who enjoys building momentum.

  • Un-timed Mode

    No countdown. No panic. No pressure. Just you, the board, and the next move. Un-timed Mode is where HyperBlocks becomes a calm, almost meditative experience. You can think, place, clear, pause, return, and keep going without being rushed.

Some days you want speed. Some days you want space. HyperBlocks understands both.
